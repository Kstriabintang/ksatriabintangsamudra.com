## The Challenge

Most music apps either lock you into one catalogue or overwhelm you with choices. I wanted a single player that could pull from multiple sources, play without gaps between tracks, and actually learn what you like — not just what you clicked once. The goal was a streaming experience that feels personal from the first visit, built entirely on the web with no native app required.

## My Approach

VenWave is built around three ideas: a **unified catalogue**, a **gapless engine**, and a **taste model**. The catalogue aggregates tracks from multiple sources into one searchable library with deduplication and source priority. The player uses the Web Audio API for true gapless playback, crossfade, and a real-time visualiser that reacts to frequency data. Every listen, skip, like and replay feeds a lightweight preference model stored per user, so the dashboard surfaces more of what you actually want to hear.

## The Design

Dark, immersive and focused on the music. A full-width player with album art, waveform visualiser and keyboard shortcuts. The dashboard adapts per visit — recently played, suggested based on your skips, and a "because you liked" rail that updates as the taste model learns. Responsive from phone to desktop, with touch-friendly controls and a collapsible queue.

## The Results

A working music-streaming app with multi-source search, gapless playback, live visualisation and a personalisation engine that improves with every session. Built with Next.js 15 App Router, Prisma and Postgres for type-safe data access. Currently in maintenance while moving to a new always-on server; the case study and demo will be back online soon.
